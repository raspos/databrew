WATCH SERIES · 5 PROJECTS

Reading the world's changes in numbers

A series that keeps watch on economic, financial and real-estate markets and builds new indices and analysis tools. Five services form one observation network.

BOK Watch

How will the Bank of Korea decide at its next meeting?

Turns KOFR futures prices into a model that shows the odds of a hold, cut or hike at the next BOK meeting on a gauge. Compare the governor's remarks, minutes, changes in statement wording and broker/IB forecast consensus on one screen.
Details Open service ↗

FX Watch

How far could the won–dollar rate move?

Calculates 50%, 80% and 90% forecast ranges for the won per dollar 7 and 30 days ahead, plus the probability of reaching a given rate. A scorecard (CRPS) shows how well the model has actually performed; it is not a directional forecast or investment advice.
Details Open service ↗

DEBT Watch

Can governments handle the debt coming due?

Shows 7-, 30- and 90-day expected yield ranges for US 10-year Treasuries and Korean government bonds, and calculates how much extra interest maturing debt costs to roll over under different scenarios. Also includes a maturity ladder of 460 securities and a world debt map.
Details Open service ↗

PAY Watch

How much do my friends earn?

Search average salaries at 5,463 companies, rank the companies your friends work for, or compare two to four companies head to head in a salary battle. Every figure shows whether it comes from a DART filing or a Saramin estimate, and for which year.
Details Open service ↗

APT Watch

How far above recent sales are sellers' asking prices?

Compares the median asking price of listings in 110 sample complexes across Seoul's 25 districts with the median transaction price of the last 90 days, shown as a negotiation-pressure index. Also tracks listing changes and a district-level market sentiment map.
Details Open service ↗

Total visits Counting…

Personnel Craft

Can you land the desk you want in this reshuffle?

Build a 3D block-style reporter, stack up your career and pick the desks you want, then spend four weeks after Chuseok meeting desk heads and spreading rumors. A fictional game in which odds calculated from 10,000 simulations decide your posting.

Hongdam Craft

As PR chief, can you survive six rounds of reporter scrutiny?

Play a PR chief at a fictional company and get through six crisis stages: exclusive, tip-off, disclosure, investigation, prosecution and owner. Reporters come in 12 types, so the same response can work on one and backfire on another, and survival odds are simulated with Monte Carlo.

Reconstruction Craft

Can you hit your target in the redevelopment bidding war?

Pick one of eight fictional builders and win over homeowner votes across 15 complexes in a turn-based strategy game. Whether you spend on information, budget or time first decides whether you hit your order target. Everything is fictional.

SPORTS SERIES · 5 PROJECTS

Reading sports through records

A series that collects and compares records and applies new analysis to the questions sports fans ask. Record halls, indices, comparisons and hands-on play sit side by side.

Son Heung-min Goal Museum

Every goal Son Heung-min scored, on record.

A complete archive of Son Heung-min's 302 career goals in top-flight club football and full internationals, each recorded with its sources. Filter by team, competition, season and opponent, and explore scoring patterns, shot locations, xG and links to goal clips.
Details Open museum ↗

E-WAR

Who beat expectations the most?

Estimates a pre-season expected WAR from the previous three seasons and age, then finds the KBO players who beat expectations by the gap to actual WAR (E-WAR). Sort the 2020-2026 seasons by team and position; the expectation is a statistical estimate, not a club's internal one.
Details Open service ↗

The Se Young Archive

Is An Se-young the greatest ever?

Organizes An Se-young's 390 recorded matches, win rate, titles and world rankings with sources, and lines them up against 15 women's badminton legends. A GOAT LAB lets you set your own criteria and pick your all-time best.
Details Open archive ↗

All-Time XI

Build the greatest XI of all time.

Pick one of the top 30 FIFA-ranked nations, choose from seven formations, build your own best eleven from its all-time national team players and share it by link or image. Includes a player database from the 1900s to the 2020s and a gallery of other users' best elevens.
Details Build your XI ↗

Chu-Kang-Dae-Yeop + Kim

Who is Korea's greatest hitter?

Converts the records of Choo Shin-soo, Kang Jung-ho, Lee Dae-ho, Lee Seung-yuop and Kim Do-yeong to a KBO basis and compares the five side by side at the same year of their careers. Converted figures are estimates and you can switch to the original records; there is also a batting game.
