BOK Watch

Reading Bank of Korea monetary policy through data

A service that estimates the next Monetary Policy Board rate decision from market data. See model probabilities backed out of KOFR futures prices, the governor's remarks and minutes, and expert forecasts on a single screen. Updated automatically every day at 10 a.m.

Try the service ↗ Intro document (.md, Korean)

BOK Watch main screen — gauge of hold and cut probabilities for the next Monetary Policy Board meeting, plus a table of expected policy rates by meeting. Captured 2026-10-15.

01 / WHY

Why we built it

The press reports that "experts expect a hold," but there was no way to know, without separate calculations, how much probability the market actually assigns to a rate change. In financial markets, that collective judgment is already built into KOFR futures prices.

We turned this into a model that presents hold, cut and hike probabilities as numbers, and lets you compare the governor's remarks, minutes and institutional forecasts on the same screen. The goal is a tool that reads monetary policy through data-based probabilities, not just a collection of expert opinions.

Model probabilities are estimates backed out of KOFR futures prices, not a definitive market forecast. The model's assumptions and limitations are disclosed within the service.

02 / FEATURES

What you can do

Rate decision probability estimates

Estimates hold, cut and hike probabilities for the next Monetary Policy Board meeting with a KOFR futures-based model and shows them as a gauge.

Expected rate outlook by meeting

See the policy rate level the market expects for each upcoming Monetary Policy Board meeting in a table.

Probability history

Check how the hold probability has changed over time in a day-by-day chart.

Policy statement wording comparison

Compare changes in the wording of each meeting's monetary policy decision statement side by side with the previous meeting. Hawkish and dovish auxiliary scores are also shown.

Governor's remarks and minutes

Review the Bank of Korea governor's public remarks and summaries of the minutes, along with dissenting opinions and the dot plot.

Institutional forecast consensus

Collects rate forecasts from major institutions such as brokerages and investment banks and compiles them into a consensus.

Key economic indicator calendar

See the US FOMC, CPI and jobs schedules and the release dates of major Korean indicators in a calendar.

AI composite reference indicator

Provides a reference indicator of monetary policy pressure signals that AI extracts from Naver news articles.

03 / DATA & METHODS

Data and methods

ECOS (Bank of Korea)
Automatically collects the policy rate (722Y001) and the 91-day CD market rate (817Y002). Core input data for the rate model.
KRX Open API
Collects settlement prices of 3-month KOFR futures. Used to back out hold, cut and hike probabilities for each meeting from KOFR futures.
Bank of Korea official site
Collects the full text of monetary policy decision statements, the list of minutes, and the dot plot. The basis for wording-change analysis.
Yonhap News RSS
Automatically collects and filters articles related to the governor.
Manual entry (data/reports.yaml)
Institutional forecasts, excerpts of the governor's remarks and the key economic calendar are entered and managed directly in YAML.
OpenAI API
Classifies and tags Naver news articles and generates a monetary policy pressure index. Used only as an AI reference indicator.

Update cycle: runs automatically on GitHub Actions every day at 10 a.m. Institutional forecast and remarks entries are entered manually, then deployed.

04 / HOW TO USE

How it can be used

Economy and finance reporters

Check market-implied probabilities and the expert consensus on one screen before reporting on a Monetary Policy Board meeting, and use them as background for articles.

Finance and investment professionals

Monitor signals of a shift in the monetary policy direction by watching the trend in hold probability together with dissenting opinions in the minutes.

Economics and finance researchers

Review the methodology and backtest results of the KOFR futures-based probability model directly and use them as reference material.

06 / TECH STACK

Technology and how it's made
