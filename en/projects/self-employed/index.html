01 / WHY

Why we built it

Every year more than 1 million people go into self-employment and nearly 800,000 close down. Contrary to the saying "you won't know until you try," data already exists that lets you estimate fairly accurately the odds of closing once you choose an industry and an area.

By combining National Tax Service business registration and closure statistics with Korea Small Business and Market Service commercial-area data, we made it possible to check, before opening, the actual survival rate of the industry and area you chose.

Survival rates are statistical estimates by industry and area and do not guarantee the success of any individual business. Please consult an expert before deciding to start a business.

02 / FEATURES

What you can do

Explore survival rates by industry

Compare 1-, 3- and 5-year survival rates across 50-plus industries, including Korean restaurants, cafes, beauty and cram schools.

Local saturation map

See commercial-area density and businesses per resident at the neighborhood (eup-myeon-dong) level on a map.

Simulate your startup scenario

Enter an industry, area and capital, and it presents closure-rate statistics for owners in similar conditions.

Industry trend over time

Use the past five years of openings and closures to see which industries are rising and which are fading.

Why businesses close

See the distribution of closure reasons from a Small Business and Market Service survey, and how it differs by industry.

Links to support policies

Provides links to small-business startup and closure support policies.

03 / DATA & METHODS

Data and analysis methods

National Tax Service Statistics Portal
Business registrations and closures by industry and area. Yearly time-series data.
Korea Small Business and Market Service
Commercial-area analysis service data. Business density and estimated sales by industry at the neighborhood level.
Statistics Korea Census on Establishments
Five-year survey data on the number of establishments and workers by industry.
