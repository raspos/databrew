Son Heung-min Goal Museum

302 goals, recorded one by one, with sources

From Hamburg to Leverkusen, Tottenham and LAFC, and the Korea national team. A digital museum that archives the 302 goals scored in official senior professional matches and international matches, one by one, with date, opponent, how it was scored and source.

01 / WHY

Why we built it

In sports coverage, a player's scoring record is often cited only as a career total. There was nowhere to look up in which match, in what way and at what moment each goal came.

For a player like Son Heung-min, who has played for many years on multinational stages, the records are scattered and hard to check in full. We cross-verified two sources, Transfermarkt and ESPN, to organize them so each goal can be checked, and linked individual goals to YouTube videos so you can see the record and the moment together.

Scope: official senior professional matches (Bundesliga, FA Cup, UCL, PL, MLS and others) and senior national-team international matches, both finals and qualifiers. Youth national teams, the Olympics, friendlies and penalty shootouts are excluded.

02 / FEATURES

What you can do

Browse all 302 career goals

Find the goals you want with filters for team, competition, season, opponent, how it was scored and verification level.

Goals by season

Check the distribution of goals by team and the flow of his career with bar charts by year and by club season.

Scoring pattern analysis

See distributions by foot or head, time of the goal (15-minute bands), how it was scored (open play, penalty, free kick), home or away, and match result.

Shot location and xG

Using linked Understat data, see the shot locations of Premier League and Bundesliga goals on a pitch and view the xG distribution.

Detail panel for each goal

Click a goal number to open a detail panel with date, opponent, score, how it was scored, assist, xG and video source.

Linked YouTube goal videos

For goals where an official-channel highlight or goal clip has been found, you can open the video link directly.

03 / DATA & METHODS

Data and analysis methods

Transfermarkt
Raw data by goal: date, competition, opponent, time of goal, score, goal type and assist provider. Official matches only were extracted from 329 rows including youth. Primary source for confirmation.
ESPN
Cross-checked against scoring events by match. ESPN match links are connected to 275 goals. Matched on scorer, match and time (±1 minute).
Understat
Shot location, xG and situation for 138 goals in the PL 2015/16–2024/25 and Bundesliga 2014/15. Coordinates converted to a 105×68m pitch.
Official club and league sources
Totals verified against Tottenham's club records (173 goals), the official PL record (127 goals, by foot and head), the official Bundesliga record, the LAFC club and Yonhap News's record of international matches.
YouTube (official channels)
Only videos from the official channels of clubs, leagues, federations and broadcasters are linked. Unofficial channels are not included.

Three levels of verification: ① Goal level (Transfermarkt and ESPN match on match, scorer and time ±1 minute) ② Match level (a second source confirms the match and the goal) ③ Single source (confirmed only by Transfermarkt). Data as of 2026-10-15.

04 / HOW TO USE

How you can use it

Sports reporters and content creators

When producing articles or content about Son Heung-min, quickly check records against a specific opponent, competition or season and get source links.

Football fans

Look up the detailed record of a memorable goal and rewatch it on YouTube, or explore the trend of his whole career.

Sports data researchers

Browse long-career data spanning multinational stages, with sources and verification levels, and use it as a basis for analysis.

06 / TECH STACK

Tech and how it was made

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript Python (data verification) Claude Code Claude.ai Artifact

The build output is a static site made of a single HTML file (plus data JSON). It runs directly in the browser with no separate server and is deployed as a Claude.ai Artifact. The data is contained in goals.js / goals.json, and integrity is checked with a Python verification script.
