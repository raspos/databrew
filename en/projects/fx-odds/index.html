FX Watch

Reading the odds and pressure behind the exchange rate

An exchange rate monitor that uses a probability distribution to show which range the KRW/USD rate is most likely to be in 7 and 30 days from now. It doesn't assert a direction; it shows uncertainty through each model's prediction interval and the probability of reaching a given level.

01 / WHY

Why we built it

Exchange rate forecast stories usually end with a number or two, or a direction. But what can be said with confidence about the exchange rate is not the direction; it is the probability that it stays within a certain range.

Carrying on the design philosophy of BOK Watch, we built it to show the uncertainty of the exchange rate as a probability distribution, and to publish a scorecard of which models have actually fit better.

Statistical probabilities are estimated from past data and do not guarantee actual outcomes. The service states that its official models carry no directional information and have no verified directional forecasting power. This is not investment advice.

02 / FEATURES

What you can do

The exchange rate's future

See the 50%, 80%, and 90% prediction intervals and the probability distribution for the won per dollar 7 and 30 days ahead.

Probability calculator for a specific rate

Enter the rate you want and it calculates the probability of ending there at maturity and of breaching it during the period.

Volatility index (FXVI)

Shows on a 0–100 scale where exchange rate volatility stands historically, alongside the recent intraday range.

Pressure and news

Experimentally shows a news pressure index (FSI) and a statistical pressure index (FPI). They measure directional pressure, not probability.

Co-movement radar

Check how closely the won moves together with other currencies.

Forecast scorecard and methodology

Publishes each model's performance using the score (CRPS) of forecasts whose outcomes are settled, and explains the methodology.

03 / DATA & METHODS

Data and analysis methods

Bank of Korea ECOS
Time series for exchange rates, interest rates, and the balance of payments, including the won/US dollar base rate (731Y001, Seoul foreign exchange market basis).
Yahoo Finance
Overseas market indicators. Shown on the service screen with a separate reference date.
Forecast models
The official models are EWMA for 7 days and Parkinson (high-low prices, scale-adjusted) for 30 days. The expected range is calculated from exchange rate returns and volatility alone, with no directional judgment.

Qualitative pressure and the integrated outlook are not yet validated, so they are not reflected in the official forecast. Model explanations and validation results are in the service's Methodology and Forecast Scorecard tabs.
