Chu-Kang-Dae-Yeop + Kim

Reading the generational change of Korean baseball hitters through records

Choo Shin-soo, Kang Jung-ho, Lee Dae-ho, Lee Seung-yuop, and Kim Do-yeong. The official records of five hitters who played in different eras and leagues are converted to a KBO basis and compared side by side by year of career, and you can switch back to the original records too.

Try the service ↗ Intro document (.md, Korean)

Chu-Kang-Dae-Yeop + Kim main screen — player cards for the five hitters. Service reference date 2026-10-16 (end of the KBO regular season), captured 2026-10-15.

01 / WHY

Why it was made

Choo Shin-soo, Kang Jung-ho, Lee Dae-ho and Lee Seung-yuop played in different leagues in different eras. Records that passed through MLB and NPB and KBO records are hard to put on the same scale, so comparisons across generations have always relied on impressions.

With Kim Do-yeong as the hitter who follows these four, the records of the five players are converted to a KBO basis so they can be seen side by side at the same year of career. The first letters of the names (Chu, Kang, Dae, Yeop) and the Kim of Kim Do-yeong became the name of the service.

Converted records are estimates. You can switch to the original records in the service and check for yourself.

02 / FEATURES

What you can do

Player cards and abilities

See the five players as baseball cards and compare abilities such as contact, plate discipline, power, home runs, baserunning and consistency at a glance.

KBO-converted / original records toggle

Switch between viewing MLB and NPB records converted to a KBO basis and viewing the original records as they are.

Best season and year-by-year comparison

Find each player's best season and lay records side by side by which year since debut it is.

Ability and hitting analysis

Calculates abilities from hitting metrics and shows each player's characteristics.

Timeline and the Legends 12

See career flow on a timeline and browse the records of the expanded set of twelve legends.

Batting game

It doesn't stop at reading records; you can also play a batting game yourself.

03 / DATA & METHODS

Data and analysis method

KBO regular-season records
Service reference date 2026-10-16, as of the end of the KBO regular season. Uses each player's official records.
MLB and NPB records
Overseas league records are converted to a KBO basis and used in the year-by-year comparison. Original, unconverted records are also provided.

Check the service screen for the conversion method and detailed sources. This page contains only what was confirmed on the service screen.
