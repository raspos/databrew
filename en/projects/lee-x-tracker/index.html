01 / WHY

Why it was built

The president's X posts are sometimes not official positions, but because they go up in real time they are an important primary source for tracking political messages. However, X's limited search makes it hard to analyze past posts systematically.

Through AI classification, posts are organized by topic such as economy, diplomacy, welfare and politics, so you can see changes over time and emotional tone together. It can be used for fact-checking, issue tracking and compiling policy statements.

02 / FEATURES

What you can do

Browse all 1,065 posts

Filter by date, topic and keyword to find the posts you want quickly.

AI classification by topic

See the classification results by topic, such as economy, diplomacy, welfare and politics, along with the reasoning for each classification.

Post time-series analysis

View daily, weekly and monthly posting frequency and changes in topic share as charts.

AI sentiment analysis

Check the distribution of emotional tone (positive, negative, neutral) per post and how it changes over time.

Approval-rating comparison

Compare approval ratings from major polls side by side with posting patterns.

Direct links to originals

Jump straight from each post to the original on X to check the context.

03 / DATA & METHODS

Data and analysis methods

X (Twitter) API
Collects posts from President Lee Jae-myung's official X account, including text, date, likes, retweets and links.
OpenAI API (GPT-4)
Topic classification and sentiment analysis for each post. Classification scheme: five categories — economy, diplomacy, welfare, politics and other. Prompts and classification criteria are public.
Polling data
Weekly approval-rating survey results from major organizations. Used only for comparative analysis with posting patterns.

AI classification results are automatic and may contain errors. The classification criteria and prompts are published within the service.

04 / HOW TO USE

How it can be used

Political reporters

Quickly check what the president said on a given issue or date and secure a link to the original.

Fact-checkers and researchers

Track the statement archive over time to analyze shifts in position or patterns.

Readers interested in politics

Through posts organized by topic, get a clear picture of the president's interests and how his messages have changed.
