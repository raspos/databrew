01 / WHY

Why we built it

The Nobel economics prize is announced every year, but unless you are a specialist, it is hard to grasp what the laureates' research is and why it matters. The names are familiar; the theories are not.

Built on the official prize records, this archive connects researchers to their works and sets the economic questions of their time next to today's. It is an attempt to show the path from one person's theory to the questions of the world economy.

The Nobel economics prize does not diagnose the economy of a given year; it honors research that contributed to economic science. Field explanations are editorial commentary, and the reasons for each award are marked separately from interpretation.

02 / FEATURES

What you can do

Timeline of 58 prize years

Browse every prize year from 1969 to 2026. Filter by theme — growth and innovation, finance and crises, labor and inequality, institutions and poverty, macro policy, markets and information, climate — and only the connected research remains.

Official award reasons by year

Click a year to open that year's laureates and the official Nobel citation.

Profiles of 101 laureates

Search by name or research keyword to see key papers and works, a track for specialists, and official Nobel information.

Drawing mechanisms instead of formulas

Concept diagrams show the representative models that explain growth, financial crises, information, and policy, such as the Solow growth model. They are conceptual diagrams, not empirical estimates.

An evidence-based laureate network

Laureates are linked by shared awards in the same year, the same affiliation at the time of the prize, the same degree-granting school, and the same research tradition. A line is never overstated as proof of a relationship.

Statistics by people and institutions

Compare age and affiliation at the time of the prize, degree-granting schools, and nationality and birthplace on the same basis. Items that could not be confirmed are not guessed.

03 / DATA & METHODS

Data and analysis methods

Official Nobel prize records
58 prize years from 1969 to 2026, 101 laureates. Linked to the official prize page for each year.
Primary sources by person
Education, career, and nationality are included only where confirmed in primary sources for each person. Multiple degrees, affiliations, or nationalities are counted once in each category.
Editorial commentary
Field explanations and theory concept diagrams are editorial commentary, marked separately from the award reasons.

"Unconfirmed" does not mean the person has no such career or nationality. Rankings are affected by how much data could be obtained; age is full years as of the announcement date, and country of birth is not the same as nationality.
