01 / WHY

Why we built it

Corporate rankings are usually ordered by revenue or assets. But what investors and readers want to know is not current size but which companies are actually getting better.

We measure three years of change from financial statements filed with the Financial Supervisory Service's OpenDART, to find "not the biggest companies, but the ones that changed the most."

The current ranking is provisional. Checks of original filings and amended filings, consolidated versus separate statements, market and industry classification, and outlier validation are still under way, so results may change. Scores are relative evaluations within a comparison group, not absolute ones.

02 / FEATURES

What you can do

Corporate Momentum Index (CII)

Measures financial improvement over 2022–2025. A provisional ranking is published for companies with complete data.

Financial Strength Index (FSI)

Evaluates 2025 profitability, financial stability, cash generation and asset efficiency, shown separately from momentum.

Turnaround companies

Browse companies whose finances are currently weak but that have shown a recent recovery, gathered in one place.

Corporate momentum map

Places companies on FSI (horizontal) and CII (vertical) coordinates so you can compare strength and speed of improvement at a glance.

Size groups and special lenses

Explore by groups based on 2024–2025 average total assets, and by special lenses such as revenue growth and return to profit.

Company head-to-head and download

Compare up to three companies side by side, and download the results as CSV or Excel.

03 / DATA & METHODS

Data and methods

Financial Supervisory Service OpenDART
Financial statements filed by listed companies. The raw data for calculating growth, profitability, financial soundness and cash generation indicators.
Momentum Index (CII)
Measures improvement over 2022–2025. Converted to relative scores within a comparison group.
Financial Strength Index (FSI)
Evaluates 2025 profitability, financial stability, cash generation and asset efficiency. Reported separately from CII.

Size groups are evaluation bands based on 2024–2025 average total assets and are not a legal classification of company size. 2026 quarterly figures are not included in the 2025 annual evaluation. The methodology, model validation and data collection quality status are published within the service.

04 / HOW TO USE

How it can be used

Economy and corporate beat reporters

Find companies worth watching based on change rather than size, and use them as a starting point for checking against the original filings.

Investors and analysts

Look at momentum and financial strength separately to screen turnaround candidates, and compare companies head-to-head.

General readers

Check on the map whether the finances of a company you only know by name have actually improved over the past three years.
