E-WAR

Players who beat expectations

How much did the club expect, and how much did the player deliver? E-WAR is a KBO player's actual WAR minus the pre-season expected WAR, used to find players and clubs that delivered more than expected.

01 / WHY

Why we built it

A WAR ranking shows who did the most, but it puts players with low expectations and players with high expectations on the same scale. What fans and clubs really want to know is how much more a player delivered than expected.

We estimate the pre-season expected WAR from the previous three seasons' records and age, and define the gap from actual WAR as E-WAR, so players who beat expectations can be found at a glance.

The WAR in this service is an approximate WAR (aWAR) calculated directly from KBO basic statistics, and is not an official WAR such as STATIZ's. Expectations are statistical expectations estimated from past records and age, not the club's actual internal expectations. Defense and baserunning are not included in WAR.

02 / FEATURES

What you can do

E-WAR ranking

Pick a season from 2020 to 2026 and sort players who beat expectations by club, position, age and playing-time criteria.

Expectation vs. reality

See expected WAR, its 90% expected interval and actual WAR side by side, with players outside the interval flagged.

Club report card

Adds up performance relative to expectations for each club and compares them.

Player types

Classifies players by type, such as above or below expectations, for browsing.

Negotiation helper

Calculates estimated surplus value and WAR per 100 million won from salary and performance, offered as reference material for salary negotiations.

Model validation lab

Publishes the error and validation results of the expectation model.

03 / DATA & METHODS

Data and methods

KBO basic statistics
Approximate WAR is calculated by adding a positional adjustment to batting for hitters, and by blending FIP and runs allowed per nine innings (RA9) for pitchers. The replacement level is calibrated to Naver WAR.
Expected WAR
Estimated from the previous three seasons' records and age. For the 2026 season it combines the model expectation with salary-converted WAR, with calculated weights of 75% model to 25% salary.
Salary
Only 2026 season salaries were obtained, with no contract (FA) information. Foreign players' dollar salaries are converted at the average exchange rate.

The ranking covers 270 players (2026) who have records from the previous three seasons and exceed the minimum playing-time threshold; rookies and returning players are excluded because expectations cannot be built for them. The service states that no backtest could be run because there is only one season of data.
