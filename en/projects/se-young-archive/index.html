01 / WHY

Why we built it

"Is An Se-young the greatest ever?" is a question both fans and the press ask, but answers often lean on impressions and reputation.

To judge by records rather than reputation, we organized An Se-young's matches, win rate, titles and world ranking with sources, and made it possible to set them beside 15 legends.

The service states that the number of matches it includes can differ from the total career match count. All figures are the values shown on the service's screens (data as of 2026-10-27).

03 / DATA & METHODS

Data and analysis methods

Match records
390 matches fully recorded through 2026-10-25: 334 wins, 56 losses (win rate 85.6%).
Titles
One Olympic gold medal (Paris 2024, Olympedia), two World Championship titles (2023 and 2026, confirmed by OCA).
World ranking
No. 1 as of 2026-10-27. First reached world No. 1 on 2023-08-01 (citing KBS and the federation's announcement). 478 records linked after verification by combining 106 collected tables with pre-match and year-end values.

Periods with no collected table are marked in the service's chart as weeks with no linked table. The world ranking is shown with collected tables as solid lines, pre-match observations as dotted lines, and year-end reference values as standalone dots.
