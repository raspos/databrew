The Science
of K-Pride

Korea's world rankings, measured with 53 official statistics

Explore interactively Korea's world rankings, measured with 53 official indicators from international organizations such as the World Bank, OECD, WIPO, UNDP, IFR, and IOC. It also offers a 50-year timeline, country comparisons, and a quiz.

01 / WHY

Why we built it

The question "Where does Korea rank in the world?" is often tossed around without context. Korea's economy is among the largest, yet it is also near the bottom in working hours, inequality, and suicide rate. Until you see all the indicators on one screen, it is hard to tell which side is the "fact."

We selected 53 indicators using only official sources from international organizations, and attached context explanations so they are not quoted selectively or interpreted arbitrarily. The title "K-Pride" (gukppong) means we should examine both excessive pride and excessive pessimism about Korea.

Data sources: only data officially published by the World Bank, OECD, WIPO, UNDP, IFR (International Federation of Robotics), IOC, WHO, ILO, and IMF is included. The number of countries compared varies by indicator.

02 / FEATURES

What you can do

Explore 53 indicators

Filter by category — economy, technology, society, environment, sports — to see Korea's rank and value for the indicator you want.

Country comparison

Select up to 5 countries and compare them side by side across all 53 indicators. Filters based on G7 and OECD membership are also available.

50-year timeline

See, year by year from 1973 to 2023, how Korea's rank changed on which indicators.

World map visualization

For each indicator, each country's rank and value are shown in color on a world map. Built on D3 Geo and TopoJSON.

Ranking quiz (28 questions)

An interactive quiz where you guess Korea's rank on 28 indicators, to find out "how much do I really know?"

Transparent sources

Every indicator shows a link to the original source at the international organization and the latest reference year of the data.

03 / DATA & METHODS

Data and analysis methods

World Bank
Macro and social indicators such as GDP, trade volume, R&D investment, internet penetration, life expectancy, and infant mortality.
OECD
Average annual working hours, income inequality (Gini), education spending, elderly poverty rate, suicide rate, and life satisfaction.
WIPO
Global Innovation Index (GII), number of patent applications, and trademark and design registrations.
UNDP
Human Development Index (HDI), Gender Inequality Index (GII), and Multidimensional Poverty Index.
IFR (International Federation of Robotics)
Manufacturing robot density (units installed per 10,000 workers).
IOC, FIFA, and others
Summer Olympics medal rankings, FIFA rankings, and esports tournament results.

All data includes only raw data officially published by international organizations; secondarily processed statistics are not used. The data reference year varies by indicator (see each indicator's detail screen).

04 / HOW TO USE

How to use it

Economy and society reporters

Check Korea's rank on a specific indicator in an international comparison and get the official source right away.

Education and research

Explore international comparison indicators interactively in social science and economics classes and understand 50 years of change.

General readers

Use the quiz to see how accurately you actually know Korea's rankings.

06 / TECH STACK

Technology and how it was made

Public APIs and download files from international organizations are cleaned with a Python pipeline and converted to JSON. It is rendered with Next.js and Tailwind CSS 4 and deployed on Vercel.
