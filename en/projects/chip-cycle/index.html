CHIP CYCLE

Export value surged, but the number of units sold fell

Twenty years of Korean semiconductor exports, tracked with 237 months of customs data on DRAM, flash memory and multi-chip packages and the Bank of Korea export price index. A Shapley decomposition shows whether the surge in export value came from higher volume or higher prices.

01 / WHY

Why we built it

News of record-high semiconductor exports keeps recurring. But it is rarely explained whether the rise in export value comes from selling more or from higher prices.

In September 2026, DRAM export value rose +532% from a year earlier, while declared quantity grew by only +1.5%. Almost all of the export growth came from rising unit prices. We built this to confirm and explain that structure directly from customs data.

Data range: January 2007 – September 2026 (237 months, 711 observations in total). HSK codes for the three main items: DRAM 8542321010, flash 8542321030, multi-chip package 8542323000.

03 / DATA & METHODS

Data and methods

Korea Customs Service / K-stat (KITA)
Raw monthly export value (USD) and declared quantity (U) by 10-digit HSK item. Collected from the Korea International Trade Association trade statistics system.
Bank of Korea ECOS
Export price index 4.3.1.3 (semiconductors, 2020=100). Used to cross-check unit price estimates.
Bank of Korea Issue Notes
Macro analysis reports No. 2026-16 and No. 2026-20. Used to analyze how semiconductor exports pass through to the terms of trade and GDI.
Analysis methodology
Decomposition of export increases: Shapley symmetric decomposition (price effect = ΔP × average Q, volume effect = ΔQ × average P). All formulas are published within the service.

Data integrity: passed the automated validation script covering 12 major checks. Data reference date: September 2026 (latest observation).
